# All Story of France (English)

This document gives an accessible, chronological overview of the history of France from prehistory to the present, with a focused section on the French Revolution. It is provided in English as part of multilingual support for the repository.

## Summary
France has been central to European history for more than two millennia. Its story includes Celtic Gaul, Roman rule, the rise of the Frankish kingdoms, the medieval Capetian monarchy, cultural growth during the Renaissance, the crisis of the Ancien Régime, the French Revolution, the Napoleonic era, 19th-century political change, two world wars, decolonization, and modern republican democracy.

## The French Revolution (concise)
Causes: social inequality under the Estates system, fiscal crisis, Enlightenment ideas, and political mismanagement by Louis XVI.

Key events: Estates-General and National Assembly (1789); Tennis Court Oath; Storming of the Bastille (14 July 1789); Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen; abolition of feudal privileges; trial and execution of Louis XVI (1793); Reign of Terror (1793–1794); Thermidorian Reaction; rise of Napoleon (1799).

Consequences: End of feudal privileges, spread of republican ideas, secularization of the state, and a long-lasting influence on modern notions of rights and citizenship.
